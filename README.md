FNB Sales Analysis Case Study
Project Overview

This project analyzes historical FNB sales data to identify revenue trends, profitability, and business performance. The objective was to clean and transform raw data using SQL, perform exploratory data analysis (EDA), create an ETL pipeline, and develop interactive dashboards to support business decision-making.

Objectives
Clean and prepare raw sales data.
Perform Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
Design an ETL pipeline.
Calculate key business metrics.
Build interactive dashboards.
Present insights and recommendations for executive decision-making.

Project Workflow
1. Data Exploration (EDA)

The dataset was explored to:

Check for missing values
Identify duplicate records
Verify data types
Generate summary statistics
Understand data distribution
2. ETL Process

A cleaned dataset (fnb_sales_clean) was created by calculating:

Gross Profit
Unit Price
Cost per Unit
Gross Profit Percentage
Gross Profit per Unit

A Gold Layer (fnb_sales_gold) was then created with additional business features:

Running Profit
Average Unit Price
Year
Month
Month Name
Quarter
Day of Week
3. Dashboard Development

Interactive dashboards were created to monitor:

Total Sales
Total Cost of Sales
Total Gross Profit
Gross Profit Margin
Average Unit Price
Monthly Sales Trends
Gross Profit Trends
Quarterly Performance
Key Business Insights
Total Cost of Sales exceeded Total Sales, resulting in an overall loss.
Gross Profit remained negative throughout the reporting period.
Certain months generated significantly higher sales than others.
Quarter 2 contributed the largest portion of total gross profit despite remaining negative overall.
Operating costs require immediate review to improve profitability.
Recommendations
Review pricing strategy to improve profit margins.
Reduce operational and procurement costs.
Investigate high-cost months and implement cost controls.
Focus marketing efforts during high-performing months.
Monitor profitability using interactive dashboards for ongoing decision-making.
